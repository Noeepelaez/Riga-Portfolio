ABOUT ME

The person behind the lens

Hi, I'm Noé Peláez Miranda

I am a Software Engineering student from Universidad de Oviedo, currently experiencing my Erasmus exchange at Vilnius Business College in Lithuania.

Whenever I can, I like to step away from the screen and travel. Living in the Baltics for my Erasmus is giving me an amazing opportunity to wander through unknown streets and discover different cultures.

This portfolio is a visual diary of my first weekend getaway to Riga. From its incredible Art Nouveau architecture to its quiet streets at night, these are my favorite memories from the trip.
